Control your hero and living shadow as one—yet not always the same way. Shadows Shift Survival is a dual-control survival challenge through deadly traps, shifting platforms, spinning blades, laser hazards, and shadow gates. Every move counts: time your actions, dodge collapsing paths, and reach the finish before the darkness wins.

How to play

  1. Move both forms — Guide the hero and its connected shadow through each obstacle course.
  2. Read the trap — Platforms shift, blades spin, and lasers fire on patterns you must learn.
  3. Split and sync — Hero and shadow do not always mirror each other; use both to solve gates and puzzles.
  4. Survive the run — Clear quick levels, unlock new themes, and push through rising difficulty.
